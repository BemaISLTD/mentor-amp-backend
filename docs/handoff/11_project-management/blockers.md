## Rules

- A blocker stays open until the required decision, asset, permission, or evidence is available and verified.
- A controlled assumption requires an owner, rationale, impact assessment, expiry/review date, and approval.
- Developers cannot resolve actuarial, accounting, tolerance, or expected-result blockers by assumption.
- Every blocker must link to affected requirement IDs and resulting GitHub issues once issue setup is complete.
